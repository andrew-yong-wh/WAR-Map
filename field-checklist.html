How your result is worked out

Five lenses, drawn from Sun Tzu’s five factors

Sun Tzu opens The Art of War with five factors every commander must weigh. WAR Map™ turns them into five questions. Every answer in the quiz reflects one of them.

地BattlefieldWhere

Your strategic range, from 0 to 100, shows how evenly you draw on all five lenses, adjusted for how often each lens appeared in the quiz. A strategy answers all five questions, so the wider your range, the more strategic your thinking.

80–100 Strategic60–79 Broad40–59 Focused0–39 Single-lens

Your strategic style is the lens you reach for first. A range of 80 or more makes you a Strategist. Your blind spot is the lens you reach for least, and the first box to work on.

This is a reflection tool built on WAR Map™ and Sun Tzu, designed to show you which lenses you use. It is not a psychological test. Every style is a strength, and every blind spot is your next box to work on. Quotations are from the translation by Lionel Giles (1910).
